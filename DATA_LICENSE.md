# Data terms

The files under `data/products/` and `results/` are derived from the Alibaba
`cluster-trace-gpu-v2026` trace (Li et al., OSDI 2026). The ClusterData repository
permits use of the trace for research or study purposes and asks that the OSDI 2026
paper be cited; we do not redistribute the raw trace and these derivatives carry the
same research/study-use condition.

Subject to that condition, the derived data and result tables in this repository are
released under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 (CC BY-NC 4.0).

`data/external/` contains U.S. EIA-930 data, which is in the public domain.
`data/validation/` contains numbers transcribed from published papers and public
datasets, each row citing its source; the underlying NREL/NLR dataset
(DOI 10.7799/3025227) is not redistributed.
